# NØVEX Recorder

Screen recorder built for gamers. Phone sound and mic have separate on/off switches, floating controls, high-FPS and high-resolution recording where your phone allows it, live streaming, and no watermark.

## Download
Go to the **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** page and download the newest `.apk` file.

## Install
1. Open the downloaded `.apk` file.
2. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
3. Tap **Install**, then open NØVEX Recorder.
4. Allow microphone, notifications and **Display over other apps** when asked.

Needs Android 10 or newer. If you have an older NØVEX build signed with a different key, uninstall it first.

## Features
- Separate switches for phone sound and microphone, also while recording
- Floating controls: REC, pause, mic, sound and stop
- Up to 4K and 120 fps, but only what your phone's encoder really supports
- H.264 and HEVC, adjustable bitrate
- Live streaming to YouTube, Kick or a custom RTMP server
- Multiple colour themes
- No watermark

## Known issues
- "Hide controls from video" may show a black box on some phones. If so, turn it off and use the notification buttons.
- Android 14 and newer asks for screen-capture permission on every recording. This is an Android rule.
- Games or apps that block audio capture will not record their sound.
- 120 fps and 4K depend on your phone. If it isn't supported, the app picks the best option your phone can do.

## Updates
The app checks this page for new versions and shows an update popup. Tap **Download**, then open the downloaded file to install over your current version.

## Privacy
- Recordings are saved on your phone in `Movies/NovexRecorder`. Nothing is uploaded unless you start a live stream to the destination you choose.
- The microphone and screen are only captured while you record or stream.
- The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device and ad identifiers. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- The app contacts GitHub to check for updates.
