# NØVEX Recorder: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

NØVEX Recorder ("the app") is an Android screen recorder. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What the app does and why it needs permissions

- **Screen capture:** the app records your screen only after you start a recording or live stream and approve Android's screen-capture prompt.
- **Microphone:** used only to record your voice while you record or stream, and only while the mic switch is on.
- **Phone (system) sound:** captured only while you record or stream, and only while the phone-sound switch is on. Some apps block this capture.
- **Display over other apps:** used to show the floating controls.
- **Notifications:** used to show recording status and the stop, pause, mic and sound buttons.
- **Internet:** used for live streaming, ads and the update check (see below).

## Your recordings

Recordings are saved on your own device, in the `Movies/NovexRecorder` folder. The app does not upload your recordings anywhere. They leave your phone only if you share them yourself or start a live stream.

## Live streaming

If you use the LIVE feature, the app sends your screen video and audio to the streaming address you enter (for example YouTube, Kick or a custom server). Your stream URL and stream key are stored on your device so you don't have to retype them. They are not sent to the developer. What the streaming platform does with your stream is covered by that platform's own policy.

## Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads).

## Update check

The app contacts GitHub (`github.com`) to check whether a newer version is available. This request is made like any normal web request, so GitHub can see your IP address. No other personal information is sent by the app.

## Data the developer collects

The developer does not run any server for this app and does not collect, store or sell your personal information. The third parties named above (Google AdMob, GitHub, and any streaming platform you choose) have their own data practices.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
